Game Design Concept & Pitch

Working Titel: Spoontastic

Concept Statement: 
A game where you have to bend spoons. And for every spoon you get more reputation and money allowing you to unlock upgrades to bend more spoons more efficiently
You have to decide in which direction the spoon will bend ()

Genre(s): 
A 3d topdown incremental game inspired by bills must be paid

Target audience:
Anybody who likes incremental games

Unique Selling Points:
Its satisfying but unlike other incremental games this one is about bending spoons

Visual and Audio Style:
I want it to feel like a light simple 3d model game. It needs to feel verry relaxing and comfortable (low to no anxiety or stakes)

Game World Fiction:
You are a young psychic who has to prove himself. And to do so you have decided to predict which way spoons will bend and prove to everybody that you are the real deal. The more you correctly predict which way the spoons bend the more your reputation wil increase.
Monetization:
It wil be a promotional game for the ecstatic oracle event, so monetization wil come from the people it brings in.
 
Platform(s), Technology, and Scope (brief):
Its for PC 3D and coded in Three.js, It wil take 2 weeks to first playable and 1.5 months to complete the game (rough estimate). Major risks are the artstyle being difficult to do personally and maybe expensive to commission.
It wil use:
•	Three.js for the 3d world, camera, spoons and animations.
•	JavaScript for Gamelogic, money, reputation and prestiges
•	HTML + CSS for the upgrade menu, techtree and hud
•	Rapier for the physics of the spoons
•	LocalStorage for the savegames in the browser 
•	Vite for the project setup and testing

Core Loops:
It wil be replayable because you wil be able to prestige with your reputation giving you powerful new abilities and multipliers for each new run so that you can get further

Objective and Progression:
The players objective is to earn 1 billion reputation so that you wil be respected as a psychic
It wil have a techtree that stays the same each run with upgrades that get more expensive the further you branch out . think crit multipliers larger badges of spoons (more spoons at once) money multpipliers, chain lightning, eartquakes, and other wacky upgrades I wil make up as we go along (with permission from the project leader). 
you can unlock some permanent upgrades with reputation that wil help you in the next run.
There wil be a percentage bar that keeps track how accurate your predictions are (a sum of all of the guesses you have made)
There wil be a leaderboard that shows the top 10 scores of everybody that guesed more than 50 time.

Game Systems:
It wil need a techtree system as wel as a physics system because I want the spoons to have physics. It wil also need a save system so you can stop mid run and save your metaprogression  (reputation) between sessions. It wil also need an accuracy counter te keep track of how accurate you are with your predictions.

Interactivity:
In the game the players wil swipe over the spoons in the direction they predict it to go. If they get it right then they get money (and a little bit of reputation) and if the get it wrong the spoons wil just give a little feedback(vibration or noise) and fall of the table. They wil be replaced with new spoons.
